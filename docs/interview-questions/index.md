Use this question bank for self-study or for mock interviews targeting Cloud Engineer, DevOps Engineer, Site Reliability Engineer (SRE), and Platform Engineer roles. Start at the level that matches your current responsibilities, then move upward as you become comfortable explaining tradeoffs and handling production scenarios.

The <a href="{{ '/interview-questions/quiz/' | relative_url }}">interactive quiz</a> presents one question at a time, reveals an answer guide on request, and links to official Microsoft Learn material. The lists below remain available for printing and instructor-led interviews.

The printable pages are question-only resources; use the quiz when you want answer guides and follow-up reading.

## Suggested progression

1. Complete Level 100 without external references.
2. Use Level 200 to practice operational tradeoffs and tool selection.
3. Use Level 300 for senior production scenarios and cross-layer diagnosis.
4. Use Level 400 for principal-level platform, governance, and business decisions.
